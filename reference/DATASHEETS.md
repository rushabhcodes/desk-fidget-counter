# Power calculations

BQ24074 ISET = 4.42 kΩ gives 890/4420 ≈ **201 mA** nominal (datasheet factor tolerance gives approximately 180–223 mA). ILIM = 3.09 kΩ sets roughly 500 mA if external-limit mode were selected; firmware uses the USB100 / USB500 modes. TMR = 30 kΩ gives 4 hours nominal fast-charge timer, with the device's documented tolerance and DPPM timer stretching. ITERM floats for the default 10% termination setting.

Charger capacitors are C2 = 2.2 µF / 16 V (0603) at IN and C3/C4 = 10 µF / 16 V (0805) at OUT/BAT. The larger nominal values allow DC-bias margin over the datasheet's effective-capacitance minima. Confirm effective capacitance against the selected Murata series at the operating voltage when substituting parts.

EN1/EN2 each have hardware pulldowns. Before USB configuration, input limit is 100 mA; after the host accepts the 500 mA configuration, it is 500 mA. Both high selects USB suspend. BQ24074 OUT supplies the system independently of the battery charge current, enabling proper termination while operating. Battery supplementation remains available during USB suspend.

TS uses the real pouch-mounted NTC. A 100 kΩ / 100 kΩ divider permits safe ADC monitoring, adding 200 kΩ in parallel with the NTC. CE has a 10 kΩ pull-up and firmware drives it low only when measured TS is 450–1300 mV. With 72–78 µA bias and the specified thermistor, this is a conservative subrange of the cell's 0–45°C charging window. This calculation requires verification with a temperature chamber and measured cell temperature. Charging stays disabled on open/short NTC. The chip's independent TS window remains active.

The LDO trades some cell capacity for simplicity. Firmware stops accepting motion below 3.45 V after three 100 ms readings, before 3V3 regulation or the AS5600's 3.0 V minimum becomes unreliable. The cell protector provides the separate final cutoff. Runtime, USB electrical margins, charge temperature, noise and sensor speed require measurements.
