# Rev E3 / v5.2.1 validation record

Reviewed 2026-11-06. This is a **software-checked prototype design**; physical hardware tests remain unperformed. Rev E3 exposes the original side switch on a supported front PCB tab and removes the printed plunger. It uses the display’s included cable, shortened and soldered at J6; the optional male header is DNP, leaving 38 populated parts. Its 68×83.2 mm PCB with a USB extension has a 34.6×40.6 mm R2 window for the static display harness and independent supports.

## Default population and placement

Release v5.2.1 adds the README procurement list, three bearing-diameter coupon choices and [physical-fit acceptance record](../mechanical/FIT.md), corrects stale plunger/E2 instructions, and gives the catalogue bearing envelope a steel GLB material. The eight product print geometries and all PCB copper/placements remain identical to v5.2.0. Race/shield geometry and physical measurements remain unavailable; a steel render does not establish mechanical fit. Firmware binaries are unchanged.

The board has 39 source references and 38 populated physical components. J6's optional male header is explicitly `doNotPlace`; its eight 2.54 mm-pitch plated holes terminate the shortened included display lead from above. No body is hidden or modelled as installed. The PCB pads, nets, drills and full copper checks remain present. BOM, placement export and KiCad DNP flags agree.

The direct SW1 housing/actuator and J1 socket intentionally overhang their board noses. Their solder lands and drills remain inside the board with normal edge clearance. No other component may overhang. Placement checks require zero errors and zero warnings; source/physical contracts separately require this precise population, cable termination and direct control.

## Export and artifact scope

The PCB has **two copper layers**, 1.6 mm thickness, 118 native routed PCB traces and 118 native vias. Every connection is generated by tscircuit's built-in Pipeline 9 autorouter in a single whole-board phase, using tscircuit 0.0.3077 / core 0.0.2267 / capacity-autorouter 0.0.1038 at 2x effort. There is no custom routing function, saved route replay, prescribed PCB path, manual routing via or external routing engine. The former generator and coordinate-repair files are removed.

Eight references are removed: D1, LED1, R11, R14, R15, R16, R29 and SW2. U6 still protects USB data/VBUS; NRST retains C13 with its internal pull-up, and firmware enables PA0's internal pull-up. VBAT is tied directly to VDD with C7 retained. SW1 is Omron B3U-3000P at (0,−40.9), facing −Y opposite USB. Short press selects Scroll / Count-only; a three-second hold clears/initializes the retained count. Boot starts in Count-only mode, and a long release cannot also change modes.

C8 is 10 nF 0402, paired with new C17 1 µF local VDDA bulk. C10 remains 100 nF in 0402, paired with new C18 4.7 µF VDDIO2 bulk. Compact bypasses and aligned return pads let native routing meet every checked 4 mm bypass limit. C8/C10 body ink is omitted where it crowds the nearby bulk-capacitor ink, and the mode label clears U4. Rev E3 translates J1, U6, C1, R1 and R2 by +9 mm in Y and adds an integral PCB tab. The complete two-layer board is freshly routed by the native autorouter; local MCU bypass placements are tightened while retaining every 4 mm trace-length limit. See [the full component audit](../reference/component-audit.json).

All 38 populated components remain top mounted. Firmware is version 5.0.0; its button behavior, internal pull-up and removed PA1/PA6 controls support the pin-compatible Rev E and Rev E3 boards. The firmware source and binaries are unchanged in hardware release v5.2.1. Previous FRAM journal records remain compatible.

Power/ground request nominal 0.19 mm routing with 0.10 mm minimum neck-downs. Native vias use Ø0.60 mm pads / Ø0.20 mm drills. Physical copper clearance remains 0.15 mm and board-edge clearance remains 0.30 mm. The native routing phase requests 0.16 mm trace/pad and 0.25 mm via/pad clearance as a numerical margin. The source's window and USB locating-hole keepouts block copper on both layers. The native phase requests 0.80 mm via drill-edge spacing as a numerical margin over the board's retained 0.55 mm requirement; fabrication minimum remains 0.50 mm.

The KiCad finalizer starts from a fresh circuit-JSON export, checks its population against the source, maps source keepout placement permissions, assigns distinct priorities to same-net ground-pour fragments, refills/removes isolated ground copper and shortens MCU corner silkscreen strokes. An exact before/after signature checks every native trace and via, including its net, layer, coordinates, width and drill. None is moved, deleted, widened or rerouted during finalization. DRC passes with zero violations, zero unconnected items and no exclusions before Gerbers are plotted. See [manufacturing](../manufacturing/README.md) and [native routing](../routing/README.md).

## Mechanical values and physical limits

The real C165948 socket face is at Y42.35003 mm and its axis is at z5.85 mm. It projects 0.15 mm beyond the side wall and sits 0.05 mm behind the wider base floor, compared with the previous 9.65 mm recess. The PCB tab ends at Y41.1 mm, leaving 1.25 mm behind the socket mouth. A continuous 13.4×6.9 mm plug-body approach prism is checked against the PCB, all 38 populated packages, prints and complete rotor sweep. Its cross-section includes the L-com drawing’s nominal 12.9×6.4 mm dimensions plus general ±0.5 mm tolerance; its 35 mm length is a checking volume, not a claimed cable dimension. The conservative shoulder plane is placed at the socket mouth; actual insertion and cable mating details still require a prototype. See [USB comparison](../mechanical/usb-access-comparison.png) and [source drawing](../reference/DATASHEETS.md).

The grip begins at z10.8 mm; overall height remains 22.3 mm before feet. Nominal clearances include 0.6 mm bezel/grip radial clearance, 1 mm optical-skirt clearance on each sensor side, 1.50 mm above the tolerance-expanded 6.9 mm USB plug envelope and 0.2 mm front-glass bezel relief. The included cable is shortened; no new harness wire or contacts are bought. Its drawn Ø1 mm insulation and PH plug are provisional design envelopes. Waveshare publishes no stock wire OD, conductor size or bend limit. The 2 mm dressing bend and per-wire modelled spans around 35.5 mm are layout values requiring measurement and adjustment. These are CAD values, not measurements.

The screen geometry is a rigid, unscaled transform of Waveshare's official STEP. Package meshes come from the documented KiCad/supplier sources; the sensor, bearing and crimp details have explicit simplifications. Sources and rights are recorded in [vendor CAD provenance](../mechanical/vendor/README.md). The real button STEP is tessellated with coincident seam topology preserved, without scaling or moving its vertices. A 12 mm fingertip sphere at the maximum depressed position clears the PCB, case and swept rotor, and contacts only the original actuator. The native 1.7×1.0 mm actuator is at Y−42.85 and Z4.8; projection past the stationary wall is 0.65 mm released / 0.25 mm at maximum 0.40 mm pretravel. The base supports the tab below and two lid pads restrain it above. No printed plunger or cap remains. The 84.8×22.3 mm body plus actuator has about 85.25 mm total depth. Actual tolerance, tactile force and accessibility remain unmeasured.

The former PH-to-PH custom harness did not match the board end of Waveshare’s included jumper lead. The default E3 assembly retains the supplied display PH plug, shortens its wires and solders J6. The actual cable dimensions, dressed bends and solder-end support **must be measured before fabrication/physical-fit signoff**. The whole unmodified 20 cm cable and optional header are not fit-qualified in this low enclosure.

**Physical fit, crimp quality, printed tolerances, 950 nm opacity, optical counts, USB signal integrity/inrush/suspend current and real macOS/Windows/Linux USB operation remain untested.** The serial smoke test uses a PTY simulator, not fabricated hardware. The companion must run to provide laptop metrics; stale data disappears after five seconds. USB suspend blanks the screen and turns the optical emitter off, so rotation during host suspend is not counted. No hardware has been ordered or physically tested.
