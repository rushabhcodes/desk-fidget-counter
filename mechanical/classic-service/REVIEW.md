# Classic F1 service enclosure review

| Iteration | Finding | Change | Recheck and status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · Reduce printed parts | The carrier and bezel shared the same two screws. Joining the two created a small contact with the supplied LCD solid under the retaining lip. | Combined the shroud, retaining face and bosses. Relieved the lip with the actual LCD geometry and reserved 2 mm for rear components. | **Resolved in CAD.** All four display bosses seat, the two screw bores pass through, and all 144 sampled radial sight lines are covered. The 0.8 mm grip seam is retained. |
| 2 · Service without separating the bearing | The top-entry chassis screws are hidden beneath the fitted grip. The first enclosure held the F1 board at its nominal position, but the original guide caught its front tab during removal. | Reversed the two chassis screws to install from underneath. Cut the base's PCB lift channel from the actual board outline. | **Resolved in CAD.** Screw, shank and driver gauges clear their paths. The retained grip, bearing and carrier lift together after removing the display. Populated-board and display removal paths were checked at multiple heights. |
| 3 · Preserve the control and verify exports | Opening the PCB lift channel also removed part of the hard stop for the original button. Comparing every pre-simplification Boolean vertex with a finished STL flagged vertices eliminated by the simplification. | Moved the inward stops into the removable rotor carrier. Verified the solid after STL export and compared the delivered GLB against that exact STL. | **Resolved in CAD.** Thirteen full-stroke and 24 off-centre button poses clear the fixed parts. The stops and roof still capture the original cap. All five prints export as single watertight parts and match the assembled GLB. |
| 4 · Visual check of the exploded view | The first assembly export displayed white PCB faces because the CAD runtime lacked image support. | Added Pillow to the rebuild command and required both board textures during export. | **Resolved in the GLB.** Both F1 board faces retain their source pixels, mesh and position. |

The F1 PCB outline, all 52 CAD package positions, and the Classic firmware and routing were reused. The generator also tests the full rotor clearance envelopes, USB plug gauge, nominal H1 cable route with Ø0.8 mm wire, and both bearing race interfaces. Physical cable dressing, print variability, button force and travel, and bearing retention remain open for a first build.
