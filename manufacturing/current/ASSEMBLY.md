# Assembly — E13 enclosure / RP2040 F1 PCB

The product uses **six printed parts and four screws**: two DIN 965 M2×6 chassis screws and two M2×10 screws shared by the screen carrier and bezel. The complete 6712ZZ bearing is one purchased unit and one CAD object. The grip includes its optical track; there are no separate bearing clamps, lower retainer or skirt adhesive joint.

1. **Calibrate and print.** Start with the bearing-fit coupon and the actual 60×75×7 mm bearing. Use the supplied print orientations and [fit guide](mechanical/FIT.md). Print the six E13 product parts in PLA; test the integral grip/track for opacity at 950 nm. Deburr slots and fit surfaces without changing the optical track radius. Use six perimeters and 35–50% infill as starting settings; inspect layer bonding and pre-tap screw pilots. The lid needs supports beneath its annular plate; keep supports off bearing mating surfaces.
2. **Program and test the PCB before closure.** Connect the SWD service lead at J4, power the board over USB and flash RP2040 firmware 6.0.0, using SWD or ROM UF2 recovery. Then unplug power and the service lead. All 52 fitted components face upwards. J6 is the fitted keyed JST-SH8 side-entry SMT socket. Use the separately ordered H1 crimped SH8-to-PH8 harness; match circuit numbers and test continuity/isolation. The kit jumper lead is unused. [Wiring and continuity checks](manufacturing/WIRING.md).
3. **Fit the PCB in the base.** Align rear USB-C with +Y and the front button with −Y, then seat the four locating holes on the pegs. The two braced screen-support walls pass through the routed window. Trim underside leads/joints to ≤0.8 mm and insulate the inspected solder wells with one layer of the specified polyimide tape. Keep the button actuator and USB mouth unobstructed.
4. **Close the chassis with two screws.** Attach six PORON pads to the lid using 0.05 mm 467MP: four Ø4 mm at (±30, ±15) mm and two Ø2.6 mm at (±6, −35) mm. Tap the pilot holes first. Make the two button-return stacks from four 2×1.6 mm PORON pieces, with 0.05 mm adhesive between layers and against the fixed backings; leave their front faces unbonded. Drop the curved `button-cap` into the guides with the foam behind its ears and the small boss toward SW1, then install the lid with the two M2×6 screws. See the [pad placement and calibration](mechanical/FIT.md#captive-front-button). Check free motion, electrical closure before the stop and return to the released position. Do not preload the switch or use the shell to force a binding cap. J4's service lead must be removed before the bearing/rotor is fitted.
5. **Install bearing and grip with light hand pressure.** Seat the complete bearing in the grip pocket, then lower it onto the stationary post while guiding the integral optical track into the sensor's 3 mm fork. Use the calibrated push fits; stop if force is needed. Do not hammer or use the screws to force a bearing fit. Verify that the grip stays seated during normal handling and rotates freely through full turns. Physical pull-off retention and drag must be checked on the prototype.
6. **Fit the screen and its two shared screws.** Place `screen-carrier` on the braced screw supports. Orient the screen tab toward −X and mate H1’s PHR-8 display plug sideways along −X before lowering the module. Seat all four rear mounting bosses on the two wide support walls; align the two short locating pins with their matching diagonal bores. The pins locate the module and are not screw threads. Plug H1’s single SHR-08V-S-B housing into J6. Dress the eight crimped wires through the fixed cavity with their modelled slack, clear of the rotating track. Do not pull on the wires or force a housing; disconnect both plugs before lifting the screen. Install `screen-bezel`; its two M2×10 screws pass through the carrier into the braced screw supports. Check both carrier bores are fully open Ø2.2 mm. Tighten gently until the plastic supports meet, keeping pressure off the glass. These two screws secure both carrier and bezel; no extra carrier screws are used.
7. **Bring up the complete device.** Recheck unobstructed rotation, wire clearance, USB insertion and button access. Initialize FRAM deliberately, verify clockwise/counter-clockwise counts and scrolling, and run the [first-build checks](validation/BRINGUP.md). Optional feet add 1 mm to the nominal 19.5 mm height.

The fixed screen, display cable and PCB do not rotate. The PCB has top-side assembly, a 34.6×40.6 mm window and accessible side ports. The body is approximately Ø89.4×19.5 mm. No battery or toggle is fitted. The fixed guides and lid roof stay inside the circular shell. The R44.5 mm moving face follows the circular wall and stays 0.2 mm inside its outline at rest. F1 replaces the MCU and routes with RP2040 circuitry while preserving the board outline and switch placement. E13 increases the body diameter by 4.6 mm to contain the curved face. Use the matching E13 prints with the matching F1 board.

The screen uses the official Waveshare STEP without rescaling. The bearing's 60×75×7 mm envelope is published; its internal details and unpublished wire dimensions use the accepted approximations. CAD checks cover nominal geometry and the stated tolerance envelopes. Actual printed fit, retention, optical opacity and electronic operation still require prototype verification.
